Legal writing is an important part of developing research, analytical and communication skills. Law Lex Mind provides a platform for students and aspiring legal researchers to share their ideas and engage with legal issues.

Our publication section aims to encourage thoughtful discussion on legal concepts, judicial decisions, contemporary legal developments and emerging issues.

BEFORE SUBMITTING

Publication Guidelines

01. Original Work

Submissions should be original work of the contributor and should not knowingly infringe the rights of another person.

02. Legal Research

Articles should demonstrate appropriate legal research, reasoning and analysis.

03. References

Important authorities, statutes, judgments and other sources should be properly acknowledged.

04. Review

Submitted material may be reviewed before publication on the platform.
