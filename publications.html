Contributions may cover legal concepts, legislation, judicial decisions, contemporary legal issues, policy questions and other relevant areas of law.

SUBMISSION PROCESS

How to Submit Your Work

01. Prepare

Prepare an original and well-researched legal contribution.

02. Review

Check your work for accuracy, structure, originality and proper referencing.

03. Submit

Submit your contribution through the official submission form.

04. Consideration

Submitted work may be reviewed before publication.

Before Submitting

  • Submit original work.
  • Ensure that the article is properly structured.
  • Use reliable legal authorities and sources.
  • Check spelling, grammar and formatting.
  • Properly acknowledge sources and quotations.
  • Avoid plagiarism and unauthorised reproduction of copyrighted material.
  • Ensure that the submitted work complies with the applicable submission requirements.
