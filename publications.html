Publish With LexMinds India

LexMinds India provides students and aspiring legal researchers with an opportunity to develop their writing and research skills by contributing meaningful legal content.

Contributors may submit original legal articles, case commentaries, blogs and research-based pieces for consideration.

Legal Articles

Legal Blogs

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

Before You Submit

Originality

The submitted work should be original and should not improperly reproduce another author's work.

Legal Accuracy

Authors should ensure that legal provisions, case names and citations are carefully checked before submission.

Clear Writing

Articles should be structured clearly and written in an accessible academic style.

Review

Submitted content may be reviewed before publication.
