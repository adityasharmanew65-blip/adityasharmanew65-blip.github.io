PUBLICATION OPPORTUNITY

Share Your Legal Research

Law Lex Mind encourages students, researchers and aspiring legal writers to develop their research and writing skills by contributing original legal work.

Contributors may submit legal articles, research papers and case commentaries dealing with legal concepts, legislation, judicial decisions, contemporary legal issues and developments in law.

All submissions are subject to review before publication.

SUBMISSION CATEGORIES

What Can You Submit?

⚖️

Legal Articles

Submit analytical and informative articles discussing legal principles, laws, judgments, legal developments and contemporary issues.

📚

Research Papers

Submit original academic research dealing with legal, constitutional, social, political or interdisciplinary issues.

🏛️

Case Commentaries

Analyse important judgments and discuss their facts, legal issues, reasoning, principles and wider significance.

📝

Legal Research & Analysis

Share well-researched academic work examining legislation, legal doctrines, emerging issues and developments in the legal field.

CONTRIBUTORS

Who Can Submit?

Law Lex Mind welcomes submissions from law students, researchers and individuals interested in legal research and writing.

Students from BALLB, LLB, LLM and other relevant academic backgrounds may submit their work, provided that the submission meets the required academic and originality standards.

Joint submissions may also be made where the work has been genuinely contributed to by more than one author.

AUTHORSHIP

Author & Co-Author Information

SINGLE AUTHOR
👤

Single Author

If the entire work has been researched and written by you, you should submit yourself as the sole author.

A co-author should not be added unless another person has genuinely contributed to the work.

MULTIPLE AUTHORS
👥

Co-Author

Where two or more people have substantially contributed to the research and writing, the contributors may be identified as authors and co-authors.

Please provide accurate author information in the submission form.

BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

Submission Guidelines

01. Originality

Submit original work prepared by the author or authors. Do not submit copied or plagiarised material.

02. Legal Accuracy

Ensure that legal provisions, judgments, authorities and other legal references are accurately presented.

03. Research

Submissions should demonstrate meaningful research, analysis and understanding of the chosen subject.

04. Author Information

Provide the correct name, academic information and contact details of the author or authors.

05. Review

Submission does not automatically guarantee publication. Submitted work may be reviewed before publication.
