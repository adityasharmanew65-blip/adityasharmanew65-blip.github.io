PUBLICATION OPPORTUNITY

Share Your Legal Research

Law Lex Mind welcomes original and well-researched legal content from law students, researchers and individuals interested in legal studies.

The purpose of this initiative is to encourage students to develop their legal research, analytical and academic writing skills while providing them with an opportunity to showcase their work.

Contributors may submit legal articles, research papers and case commentaries for consideration.

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

Before You Submit

1. Originality

The submitted work should be original and should not contain substantial copied material from another source.

2. Legal Research

Submissions should demonstrate appropriate legal research, reasoning and analysis.

3. Citations

Authors should properly acknowledge and cite sources, legislation, judgments, books, articles and other materials used in their work.

4. Quality

Submissions should be clearly written, logically organised and relevant to the chosen legal topic.

5. Review

Submitted work may be reviewed before a publication decision is made.

6. Author Responsibility

Authors are responsible for the accuracy, originality and authenticity of their submitted work.

HOW IT WORKS

Publication Process

01. Prepare

Prepare your original legal article, research paper or case commentary.

02. Submit

Complete the submission form and provide the required information about your work.

03. Review

Your submission may be reviewed for relevance, originality, quality and suitability.

04. Decision

The submitted work will be considered for publication based on the review process.

05. Publication

Selected work may be published through the Law Lex Mind platform.

06. Share Your Work

Published contributors can use their work as part of their academic and professional portfolio.
