OUR PUBLICATION PLATFORM

Give Your Legal Writing a Platform

Legal research is an important part of developing legal reasoning and academic skills. Writing allows students to explore legal questions, analyse authorities and communicate their understanding of the law.

Law Lex Mind encourages law students to engage in research-oriented writing and provides a platform where students can submit their original academic work for publication consideration.

Students may submit legal research papers, articles, case commentaries and other suitable legal writing through our submission form.

WHY PUBLISH?

🔎 Research Experience

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

Before You Submit

Please keep the following points in mind before submitting your work.

01. Original Work

Submitted work should be original and should not improperly reproduce the work of another author.

02. Research-Based

Submissions should demonstrate meaningful research, legal reasoning and engagement with relevant sources.

03. Proper Citations

Legal authorities and other sources used in the work should be appropriately acknowledged and cited.

04. Quality & Clarity

Submissions should be clearly written, logically structured and relevant to the chosen legal topic.
