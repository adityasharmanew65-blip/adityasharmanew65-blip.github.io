Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Law Lex Mind approaches information submitted through this website and how visitors can contact us regarding privacy-related questions.

LAST UPDATED: 2026

Privacy Policy

Law Lex Mind respects the privacy of visitors, students, researchers and other users who interact with this website.

This Privacy Policy describes the general approach followed by Law Lex Mind regarding information that users may voluntarily provide through the website.

1. Information You Provide

When you contact Law Lex Mind, submit an enquiry, or use a form provided on the website, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address and the contents of your message.

If a separate third-party form is used for article submissions, publication applications or other opportunities, the information submitted through that form may be handled according to the privacy practices of the service hosting that form.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information voluntarily provided by users may be used to respond to enquiries, communicate regarding submissions, provide requested information and manage website-related communications.

Information may also be used where reasonably necessary to maintain, protect and improve the website and its services.

3. Email Communications

Users may contact Law Lex Mind through the email address displayed on the website.

Information included in an email may be used for the purpose of responding to the sender's enquiry or providing the assistance requested.

4. Article and Publication Submissions

If you submit an article, research paper, case commentary or other material for publication consideration, information associated with that submission may be used to review and process the submission.

Submission of material does not automatically guarantee publication. Law Lex Mind may review submissions according to the applicable publication requirements.

5. Internship Opportunities

Internship information displayed on Law Lex Mind may include links to external platforms, including LinkedIn, where the latest internship announcements and application information may be published.

When you follow an external link, your interaction with that external platform is governed by the platform's own terms and privacy policy.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may contain links to third-party websites and services.

These external websites operate independently from Law Lex Mind. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of external services before submitting personal information to them.

7. Cookies and Website Technologies

The website may use basic technologies or services that support website functionality, performance or analytics.

Where third-party services are used, those services may have their own policies concerning cookies and similar technologies.

8. Data Security

Reasonable care may be taken to protect information voluntarily submitted to Law Lex Mind.

However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children and Students

Law Lex Mind is primarily an educational platform for students and individuals interested in legal education and research.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information through public forms or website communications.

10. Changes to This Policy

Law Lex Mind may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the website, services or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact Law Lex Mind.
