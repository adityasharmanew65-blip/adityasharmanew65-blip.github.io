IMPORTANT INFORMATION

About Law Lex Mind Policies

Law Lex Mind is a student-focused platform intended to support legal education, research, writing, academic development and access to information relating to internships and publication opportunities.

These policies explain the general manner in which the website operates and how users should understand the information, content and opportunities available through the platform.

By accessing or using this website, users are encouraged to read and understand the policies applicable to their interaction with Law Lex Mind.

01 — PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Law Lex Mind may receive personal information that users voluntarily provide when communicating with us, submitting academic work, applying through a form or interacting with services associated with the website.

Depending on the nature of the interaction, such information may include a name, email address, educational information, contact details, publication information or other information voluntarily provided by the user.

How Information May Be Used

Information voluntarily provided by users may be used for purposes such as responding to enquiries, processing submissions, communicating regarding publications, communicating regarding internships and managing activities associated with Law Lex Mind.

Protection of Information

Law Lex Mind takes reasonable steps to handle voluntarily provided information responsibly. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information Sharing

Law Lex Mind does not intend to sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to process a request, operate an associated service, communicate with a user or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Certain activities, including research submissions, may use external services such as Google Forms or other third-party platforms.

Information published on this website should not be considered legal advice or a substitute for advice from a qualified legal professional.

Laws, statutes, regulations, judicial decisions and legal interpretations may change over time.

Users should independently verify important legal information and, where necessary, seek professional legal advice regarding their individual circumstances.

Law Lex Mind does not guarantee that every article, note, explanation or resource available on the website will always be complete, current or applicable to every individual situation.

03 — LEGAL CONTENT

Legal Information & Educational Content

Articles, notes, explanations and other legal resources published on Law Lex Mind are intended to assist students and learners in understanding legal concepts.

Such content may discuss legislation, judicial decisions, legal principles, academic opinions and contemporary legal developments.

Educational content should not be relied upon as a definitive statement of law for a particular legal dispute or individual matter.

Readers should consult authoritative legal sources, including applicable legislation and judicial decisions, when conducting formal legal research.

04 — PUBLICATIONS

Publication Policy

Submission of Work

Law Lex Mind may accept submissions including research papers, legal articles, case commentaries and other suitable academic or legal writing.

No Guarantee of Publication

Submission of an article, research paper or case commentary does not guarantee publication.

Submitted material may be considered on the basis of relevance, originality, quality, structure, legal reasoning, research and suitability for the platform.

Originality

Contributors should submit original work and should not knowingly submit plagiarised material or material belonging to another author without appropriate permission.

Citations

Contributors are responsible for appropriately acknowledging and citing legal authorities, academic sources and other material relied upon in their submission.

Editorial Decision

Law Lex Mind may accept, request modifications to, defer or decline a submission at its discretion according to the applicable editorial process.

Author Responsibility

Authors remain responsible for the accuracy, originality and proper attribution of the work submitted by them.

Publication of an article does not necessarily mean that Law Lex Mind endorses every opinion, argument or conclusion expressed by the author.
