INTERNSHIP AT LAW LEX MIND

Learn Beyond the Classroom

Law school provides the foundation of legal knowledge, but practical exposure can help students understand how legal research, writing and analysis are applied in academic and professional settings.

Law Lex Mind aims to provide students with opportunities to participate in research-oriented and content-focused activities while developing skills that are valuable for their academic and professional journey.

Internship opportunities may involve activities such as legal research, article writing, case analysis, content development and other assigned tasks depending on the particular internship.
