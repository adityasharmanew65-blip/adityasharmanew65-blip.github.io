Law Lex Mind Internship

Build Your Legal Skills

Law Lex Mind aims to provide students with opportunities to gain practical exposure to legal research, legal writing, content creation and academic activities.

An internship with Law Lex Mind can help students develop skills that complement their academic legal education.

Internship opportunities and application announcements will be shared through our official communication channels.

LEARNING AREAS

What You Can Explore

🔎

Legal Research

Develop skills in identifying legal issues, finding relevant authorities and organising legal information.

INTERNSHIP AREAS

Areas of Work

Depending on the internship programme and requirements, students may have opportunities to work on areas such as:

Legal Research

Research legal topics, statutes, judgments and relevant authorities.

Content Development

Assist in preparing educational legal content and resources.

Research Projects

Participate in selected academic or research-oriented projects.

ELIGIBILITY

Who Can Apply?

Internship opportunities may be suitable for students pursuing law or related academic programmes who are interested in legal research, writing, education and professional development.

Specific eligibility requirements, duration, responsibilities and selection criteria may vary depending on the particular internship programme.

Applicants should carefully review the requirements mentioned in each internship announcement.
