About the Internship

The LexMinds Internship Program is an academic and skill-development initiative for law students who wish to strengthen their legal research, drafting, analytical, and writing abilities. Interns work on legal topics, case summaries, academic notes, and educational content that may be published on the LexMinds platform.

This internship is suitable for students pursuing LL.B., B.A. LL.B., B.Com. LL.B., BBA LL.B., and other law programs.

Internship announcements and application updates are published on our official LinkedIn page. Students are encouraged to follow the page regularly for new opportunities.

What Interns Will Work On

Legal Research

Research statutes, legal principles, and recent judgments.

Article Writing

Draft original legal articles on contemporary legal issues.

Case Analysis

Prepare case summaries with facts, issues, reasoning, and ratio.

Academic Notes

Create student-friendly notes and revision material.

Editing & Review

Assist in proofreading and improving legal content quality.

Digital Legal Education

Learn how legal knowledge is published and managed online.

Benefits of the Internship

  • Practical exposure to legal research methodology.
  • Improvement in legal writing and drafting skills.
  • Opportunity to publish selected work on LexMinds.
  • Certificate of Participation on successful completion.
  • Portfolio building for future internships and placements.
  • Better understanding of academic and professional legal work.

  • Professional communication.
